# Research page presentation

The current visual direction draws on OpenAI Research (https://openai.com/research/) and the Deep Research article (https://openai.com/index/introducing-deep-research/), inspected on 2026-09-29. Use centered research headings, a compact abstract, a white navigation bar, neutral pill links, and generous space around a large visual. AssemblyWorld retains its own cube logo, muted lavender scene, colored assembly parts, and warm neutral agent panel. No third-party brand assets or stylesheets are copied.

One top viewer includes fixed world axes and a Z-up ground grid. The horizontal selector contains twelve successful real cases, three per dataset; paper interaction examples remain first. Try with Your Agent directly follows the selector.

Results default to AssemblyWorldBench, then PartNet, IKEA-Manual, AssemblyBench, and Fantastic Breaks. Tabs show only dataset names. Local table data is transcribed from the corresponding AssemblyWorldBenchArXiv/tab/*.tex files, preserving numeric values, emphasis and metric qualifications without a runtime dependency on the paper repository.

Archive identifiers, hashes, timestamps and export verification notes remain internal. Licenses and asset integrity checks remain in the publication pipeline.
