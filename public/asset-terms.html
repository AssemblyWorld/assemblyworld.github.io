Asset terms & attribution

This is a non-commercial research project. The interactive industrial examples are a modified subset of AssemblyBench / Fusion 360 Gallery Dataset, not the full original dataset. Source geometry is retained; display buffers and recorded poses are derived for interactive research visualization.

Other benchmark sources

The research evaluates PartNet, IKEA-Manual, and Fantastic Breaks as well. Their original rights and access requirements remain applicable. Their geometry is not included in this public website release pending confirmation of redistribution rights.

Recorded runs

Trajectories are recorded agent runs. Playback does not execute a live agent. Qualitative examples are curated and must not be interpreted as a representative estimate of aggregate performance. Scores are final-state measurements under assembly-evaluation-v2.
